Privacy Policy – Kaj ima Zagreb

Effective Date: January 2026

Kaj ima Zagreb (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data while you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

  • The App does not collect or store any personal information from you.
  • All data shown in the App comes from official public sources provided by the City of Zagreb.
  • The App may access your device’s location to show points of interest and locations near you. This is used only locally on your device and is never stored, transmitted, or shared.

2. How We Use Location

  • Location data is used exclusively to display nearby locations on the map and provide a better user experience.
  • Location information remains on your device and is not sent to any server.
  • Accessing location is optional. You can choose to deny location permission; the App will still display all other content.

3. Data Sharing

  • The App does not share any personal data with third parties.
  • All information displayed in the App is public and sourced from official open data provided by the City of Zagreb.

4. Advertising

The App does not show ads, and does not collect data for advertising purposes.

5. External Links

The App may provide links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

6. Security

  • Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data, the risk of data breaches is minimized.
  • Location data used locally is handled securely on your device.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
